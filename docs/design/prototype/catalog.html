Component catalog

Every piece of the agent-pinball table in every state. Same CSS and JS as the table; specs are in docs/design/COMPONENTS.md.

Design tokens

Cabinet neutrals, one pair of colours per client (after the three reference tables), status inserts and context levels.

Agent bumper: status

One card per session. The ball orbits the ring while running, rests in the saucer when idle, rattles when stuck. Error stamps TILT, ended greys out to GAME OVER. Waiting holds the ball in the kickout hole: YOUR SHOT, a star insert and a frame that stays without motion.

Bumper actions

Jump to the session: open the project in the editor, copy the resume command, open the agent's app. Links only, never a write. A control is hidden when its link is null or outside the allowlist (vscode://file/, cursor://file/, codex://threads/); the strip hides when none is left.

Sound

The coin-door toggle. Its click creates the audio context, the gesture browsers require. When it was left on, the door reads ARM SOUND until the next click or key press. The chime is two short tones, at most one every 2 seconds, only on a transition into waiting.

Score reels, insert lights, drop targets

Reels roll each digit to its new value with a spring. Inserts pair colour with shape and a label. Drop targets fall one per finished turn and reset after five.

Context ramp

The detail view's gauge: a wireform ramp that fills with light, the ball riding at the current level.

Dot-matrix display

Total coins as the score, a ticker for routine events, and a full-screen takeover for the big moments: BALL SAVED, MULTIBALL, TILT, GAME OVER.

0COINS

Flippers, slingshots, plunger

Decoration that reacts to the stream: flippers flick on every event, slingshots kick on token bursts, the plunger fires when a new session starts. Click the plunger to try it.

Backglass detail

Opens from any bumper: context ramp, context history with compactions marked, coins breakdown and session facts. A native dialog, so focus and Escape behave.
